      How Daypebble works

      What is a pebble?

      A pebble is a small original puzzle you can finish in one to three minutes. There are three every day, generated for that date, so everyone in the world gets the same ones.

      Do I need an account?

      No. Your streak and scores are saved on this device. Add a nickname only if you want to appear on the daily leaderboard.

      How do friend challenges work?

      After you finish, tap "Challenge a friend". They get a link to the same puzzle with your score to beat.

      Missed a day?

      Play past days in the archive. Archive plays don't count towards your streak. Every 7-day streak earns a streak freeze (you can hold two), which automatically covers a missed day.

      What is "Call it"?

      Before each pebble you can predict your score. Call it right and you earn a 🎯 in your share, and better odds of a rare pebble when you finish all three.

      What are mystery pebbles?

      Finish all three puzzles to crack open a collectible pebble for your jar. Most are common; a few are rare, and legendary ones are about one in a hundred.
