Privacy

Daypebble stores your scores, streak and optional nickname in your browser's local storage. Nothing leaves your device unless you add a nickname, in which case your nickname, a random player id and your score are sent to our leaderboard.

We use cookieless, aggregate analytics to count visits. We do not sell data. If we add advertising, this page will be updated and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK).

To delete your data, clear this site's storage in your browser. To remove a leaderboard nickname, email hello@daypebble.com.
