Even Split

How to play Even Split

  1. All eight stones start in the left pan.
  2. Tap a stone to move it to the other pan. The beam tilts towards the heavier side.
  3. Balance both pans exactly. Your time is your score, and your move count is shown.

Tips

Add up all the stones first and halve it: that is what each pan must weigh. Place the biggest stones first, then fine-tune with the small ones.
