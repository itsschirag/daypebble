Hue Match

How to play Hue Match

  1. Use the three sliders to mix a colour that matches the target swatch.
  2. Lock in a try to see your match percentage and arrows showing which slider to move.
  3. You get three tries. Your best match is your score.

Tips

Fix the hue first, then lightness, then saturation. Our eyes are most sensitive to lightness, so small lightness errors cost the most points.

A new Hue Match puzzle appears at midnight your local time. Missed one? Try the archive.
